## 1. Recurring pain points in buying beer online

### Takeaway
The documented complaints are shipping damage and poor packing, slow or partial refunds, restocking fees and returned orders when the ID check fails, poorer browsing on general delivery apps than on specialists, drop-day site crashes, and people gaming purchase limits with multiple accounts. In Hawaii, high prices driven by ocean freight and taxes are a structural complaint, and freshness is the reason locals prefer local beer.

### Cited Findings
- **Shipping damage and packing (Tavour):** Tavour's Trustpilot TrustScore is 3.2/5. Reviews describe a crate that never arrived because it was damaged, where the customer was offered a replacement or a 50% refund and still had no refund two months later. Other reviews describe a missing shipment, a shipment with broken bottles, and "no padding whatsoever" leaving 2 of 4 cans damaged. — [Trustpilot: tavour.com](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tavour.com) [DATE UNCLEAR: rolling reviews, individual dates not retrieved]
- **Fees:** Tavour's custom crates have a flat shipping fee of $14.90 however many beers are in the crate. Shipping is free on the monthly subscription. — [Trustpilot / DraftMag summaries](https://draftmag.com/tavour-review/) [DATE UNCLEAR]
- **ID check friction and fees (Uber Eats):** If the customer has no valid ID, or doesn't match the photo, the courier returns the alcohol to the store and "you may be charged a restocking fee." — [Uber help: Alcohol delivery with Uber Eats](https://help.uber.com/en/driving-and-delivering/article/alcohol-delivery-with-uber-eats?nodeId=8f556ef3-add4-4280-8c86-af0787bb39d4) [DATE UNCLEAR: live help page]
- **Instacart Honolulu ID rules:** Customers must be 21+ and show an approved photo ID at delivery or pickup: a US driver's license, US military ID, state ID card, or US passport. — [Instacart: Alcohol delivery in Honolulu](https://www.instacart.com/alcohol-delivery/hi/near-me-in-honolulu-hi) [DATE UNCLEAR: live page]
- **Poor browsing on general apps after Drizly:** "Uber Eats is optimised for food delivery, not alcohol, and the browsing experience for spirits, wine, and beer inside Uber Eats is inferior to what Drizly offered as a specialist platform." — [Appscrip: Drizly alternatives 2026](https://appscrip.com/blog/drizly-alternative/) [DATE UNCLEAR, 2026] (vendor blog, opinion)
- **Out-of-stock and inconsistent catalog:** On Instacart, alcohol "rides along with a grocery order, pulled from whichever partner retailers near you hold the right license." On Gopuff, "the catalog is whatever that warehouse holds, and alcohol is live in many but not all of its markets." — [Saucey blog: What delivery services deliver alcohol](https://blog.saucey.com/what-delivery-services-deliver-alcohol/) [DATE UNCLEAR] (competitor blog)
- **Drop-day crashes and backlash:** "Overwhelmed brewery websites sometimes crash and the social media backlash from fans who feel wronged can be brutal." Sales that must happen at a fixed time "often result in site crashes and prevent customers with busy schedules" from getting access. — [American Craft Beer: New app feature simplifies beer lottery problems](https://www.americancraftbeer.com/new-app-feature-simplifies-beer-lottery-problems/) [OLDER: date not retrieved, likely several years old]
- **Gaming limits and bots-like behavior:** Buyers have used multiple accounts under different emails, had friends order for them, and hit purchase repeatedly to "trick the queue." Some posted photos of fingers hovering over purchase buttons on 4 phones at once. Limited releases sold out in 15–18 minutes. — [Arryved: How online sales helped Cabarrus Brewing](https://www.arryved.com/blog/how-online-sales-helped-cabarrus-brewing-company/) and related results from [Craft Brewing Business](https://www.craftbrewingbusiness.com/featured/the-online-beer-sales-revolution-has-begun-get-to-know-these-key-platforms/) [OLDER: likely 2020–2021; exact date not retrieved]
- **Hawaii price markups:** Mainland craft beer costs more in Hawaiʻi "because everything ships by container." Other drivers are shipping regulations, excise taxes and some of the highest alcohol taxes in the US. One source gives a grocery six-pack of Maui Brewing at $10–$14 against $16–$18 for a comparable mainland IPA. — [Hawaii-Guide: Hawaii craft beer guide](https://www.hawaii-guide.com/blog/hawaii-craft-beer-brewery-scene-what-to-drink) [DATE UNCLEAR]
- **Hawaii price history:** Civil Beat reported Big Island beer costing more than in Manhattan. — [Honolulu Civil Beat](https://www.civilbeat.org/2013/10/20076-living-hawaii-big-island-beer-can-cost-more-here-than-in-manhattan/) [OLDER: Oct 2013]
- **Hawaii freshness:** "Local is fresher and usually cheaper." — [Hawaii-Guide](https://www.hawaii-guide.com/blog/hawaii-craft-beer-brewery-scene-what-to-drink) [DATE UNCLEAR]

### Inferences
- Freshness and heat are a sharper risk in Hawaiʻi than on the mainland because mainland beer arrives by container. Showing canned-on dates and keeping a cold chain can set the shop apart, since the mainland options (Tavour, general apps) already draw complaints about damage and packing.
- Uber Eats' restocking fee on failed ID checks is a known annoyance. Pickup with ID checked at the counter, or pre-verified member IDs, removes it.
- Fixed-time, first-come online drops lead to crashes, multi-account gaming and public backlash. That supports The Chill Bottle Shop's model: members-first reservations or allocations before the beer arrives.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve recent (Sept 2026) Reddit, BeerAdvocate or Untappd threads about freshness and date codes, heat damage, or delivery windows. Reddit was blocked from search.
- I found no quantitative consumer survey (NielsenIQ, Circana, IWSR) on specific online beer complaints such as freshness, fees or delivery windows.
- I found no specific 2026 reports of bot or scalper incidents on beer drops. The evidence is older and anecdotal.

## 2. What consumers praise

### Takeaway
Consumers value convenience and home delivery (strong DTC demand), access to rare and curated beers (Tavour's barrel-aged catalog, bottle clubs), easy pickup and ordering ahead, member perks such as early access, and allocation systems they see as fair (lotteries and raffles that reward regulars). The taproom relationship still drives loyalty.

### Cited Findings
- **Demand for home delivery (2026 Sovos/BA DTC report):** In a Harris Poll of 2,051 US adults 21+ (703 regular craft drinkers), fielded January 2026:
  - 63% of adults and 81% of regular craft drinkers support expanding DTC beer shipping laws.
  - 72% of craft drinkers would buy more if they could have beer shipped home.
  - Would-be DTC buyers say they'd spend $104 a month (about $1,249 a year).
  - 91% would also look for those brands at retail.
  - 78% of regular craft drinkers say they're likely to buy via DTC in the future.

  — [Sovos: DtC Beer Shipping in 2026](https://sovos.com/shipcompliant/blog/dtc-beer-shipping-report-2026/); [Sovos report page](https://sovos.com/shipcompliant/content-library/dtc-beer-report/); [Brewbound on X](https://x.com/Brewbound/status/2041858103830835212); [Brewbound article](https://www.brewbound.com/news/dtc-shipping-demand-remains-high-with-beer-consumers-per-sovos-ba-report) [OLDER: report ~April 2026; survey Jan 2026]
- **Legal limits on DTC beer:** As of March 2026, brewery DTC shipping is legal in only 11 states plus DC, compared with 48 states plus DC for wine. The listed states are AK, KY, NE, NH, ND, OH, OR, VT and VA, plus limited PA and RI. **Hawaii is not among them.** — [Sovos report](https://sovos.com/shipcompliant/content-library/dtc-beer-report/) [OLDER: Mar–Apr 2026]; also [Vinetur, 2026-08-04](https://www.vinetur.com/en/20260804105227/only-11-states-allow-interstate-beer-shipping-despite-strong-consumer-demand-report-finds.html) [OLDER: Aug 4 2026]
- **Taproom and relationship loyalty (BA/Harris 2026 consumer survey):**
  - 85% of craft drinkers drink craft monthly, up 10 points and the highest since 2019–20.
  - Craft drinkers visited breweries 5.5 times on average, up from 5.1.
  - 58% bought more of a brewery's beer after a taproom visit, up from 51%.

  — [Brewers Association: Mixed Signals: 2026 Consumer Survey](https://www.brewersassociation.org/insights/mixed-signals-2026-consumer-survey/) [OLDER: 2026, exact date not retrieved]
- **Mobile ordering:** Customers use mobile ordering 40% of the time when it's offered, but still prefer ordering in person when they want questions answered or recommendations. — [Toast / RaspberryPints 2026 trend summaries](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/brewery-industry-trends-and-statistics) [DATE UNCLEAR, 2026] (vendor source)
- **Curation and rare access:** Tavour lists 2026-vintage barrel-aged releases: WeldWerks Starry Noche 2026, Fremont Rusty Nail 2026, Perennial Barrel-Aged Sump 2026 and Bottle Logic Space Trace 2026. It also runs brewery bottle clubs, for example Pure Project's 2026 "Cellar Cyndicate," shipped twice a year to select states. — [Tavour: Starry Noche 2026](https://www.tavour.com/b/starry-noche-2026-b583157); [Brewpublic: Pure Project expands 2026 bottle club with Tavour](https://brewpublic.com/beer-releases/pure-project-expands-its-2026-bottle-club-with-tavour/) [OLDER: early 2026]
- **Fair allocation and rewarding regulars:** Raffles for allocated products "reward regular customers, as compared to systems that attract bottle hunters from outside the area." Lottery entries give everyone an equal chance, with no advantage to entering early. — [MarketWatch Mag: Managing the Hunt](https://www.marketwatchmag.com/managing-the-hunt/) [DATE UNCLEAR]; [Virginia ABC lottery](https://www.abc.virginia.gov/products/limited-availability/lottery) [2026 program]
- **Member early access:** Mug club members typically get first access to pre-sales of limited releases, with first right to renew and a waitlist behind them. — [Throwback Brewery Mug Club](https://www.throwbackbrewery.com/about/mug-club/) [DATE UNCLEAR]; [The Brewer Magazine: Best practices in beer club management](https://thebrewermagazine.com/best-practices-beer-club-management/) [DATE UNCLEAR]
- **Order-ahead pickup in Oʻahu:**
  - Beer Lab HI offers online ordering with in-store pickup at its 3 Oʻahu taprooms.
  - Honolulu Beerworks has run a "Brew-Thru" with online ordering and payment for six-packs, cases and limited cans, picked up without leaving the car.
  - Village Bottle Shop (Kakaʻako), described as Hawaii's first dedicated craft beer bottle shop, has an online order page.

  — [Beer Lab HI](https://www.beerlabhi.com/); [Honolulu Beerworks](https://www.honolulubeerworks.com/pages/beerfinder); [Village Bottle Shop order page](https://www.villagebeerhawaii.com/s/order); [Yelp: Village Bottle Shop, updated Sept 2026](https://www.yelp.com/biz/village-bottle-shop-and-tasting-room-honolulu) [Yelp listing ≤30d; Brew-Thru likely OLDER, 2020-era]

### Inferences
- Hawaii is not a legal DTC beer shipping state, and mainland freight is costly. A local online shop with pickup and delivery meets demand that mainland breweries cannot serve directly. The strong national appetite for home delivery (72% would buy more) suggests there are untapped buyers on Oʻahu.
- Rising taproom loyalty and the preference for in-person advice suggest pairing online drops with community touchpoints: pickup events and staff notes on each beer.
- Oʻahu already has order-ahead pickup (Beer Lab HI, Honolulu Beerworks, Village Bottle Shop). A members-first model has to stand out on curated drops, allocation fairness and freshness, not on having online ordering at all.

### Gaps
- I found no retrievable 2026 consumer testimonials praising specific online bottle shops, and no Reddit or Untappd sentiment.
- I couldn't confirm whether Honolulu Beerworks' Brew-Thru still operates in 2026.

DoorDash, Instacart, Gopuff and specialists such as Saucey split up the demand, and critics say general apps browse alcohol worse than Drizly did. Tavour is still operating in 2026, but small. ## 4. Trade press on beer e-commerce and DTC in 2026

### Takeaway
The trade press describes craft beer as contracting: volume down about 4% in the first half of 2026 and brewery counts down about 1.8% year over year. Direct channels (taprooms, DTC, clubs) and strong demand for shipping are the bright spots. E-commerce is still a small share of beverage alcohol, and beer is a minor slice of online alcohol.

### Cited Findings
- **BA 2026 midyear report:**
  - Craft production was about -4% in the first half of 2026.
  - NielsenIQ off-premise craft was -5.2% across all channels.
  - 9,344 breweries were operating in June 2026, down 1.8% from 9,515.
  - 54% of brewers reported growth, up from 49%, with taprooms and regional breweries leading.
  - Economist Matt Gacioch said the contraction "may be moderating."

  — [BA: 2026 Midyear Report](https://www.brewersassociation.org/association-news/2026-midyear-report-pressure-persists-as-signs-of-stability-emerge/); [ProBrewer](https://probrewer.com/beverage-industry-news/ba-reports-craft-beer-down-4-at-midyear/); [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/ba-mid-year-update-more-than-half-of-craft-brewers-report-growth-despite-estimated-4-volume-decline) [likely OLDER: ~Aug 2026, exact date not retrieved]
- **[≤30d]** Brewery closures continue. For example, Old Colony Brewing in Whitman, MA closes Oct 3, 2026. — [Hoodline, Sept 2026](https://hoodline.com/2026/09/whitman-s-old-colony-brewing-closing-taps-for-good-on-october-3)
- **Market sizing (low-quality sources, use with caution):**
  - E-commerce is about 4% of beverage alcohol sales, projected to reach 6% by 2026.
  - Wine is about 70% of online alcohol sales, spirits 22% and beer about 10%.
  - 42% of US consumers order alcohol delivery more often than in 2024.
  - 43 states allow some alcohol delivery.
  - The global alcohol e-commerce market is estimated at $73.91B (2025) rising to $86.56B (2026).

  — [BigCommerce](https://www.bigcommerce.com/articles/ecommerce/alcohol-ecommerce/); [BottleCapps 2026](https://www.bottlecapps.com/blogs/alcohol-retail-trends-2026-ecommerce-rtd/); [Molson Coors blog](https://www.molsoncoorsblog.com/ecommerce-beer-trends); [The Business Research Company](https://www.thebusinessresearchcompany.com/report/alcohol-e-commerce-global-market-report) [DATE UNCLEAR]. The underlying primary data (IWSR, NielsenIQ) was not retrieved, and the figures come from vendor and market-research-reseller summaries.
- **Brewery software trend:** Brewery software now combines POS, inventory and e-commerce for click-and-collect, membership sales and online merchandise. Breweries increasingly sell online for local pickup or delivery. — [Toast 2026 trends](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/brewery-industry-trends-and-statistics); [RaspberryPints 2026](https://www.raspberrypints.com/blog/brewery-taproom-technology-trends-2026) [DATE UNCLEAR, 2026]
- **New route-to-market framing:** Drinks Business (Apr 2026) covered the Sovos/BA report under the headline "US beer industry uncovers more 'meaningful' routes to market." — [The Drinks Business](https://www.thedrinksbusiness.com/2026/04/us-beer-industry-uncovers-more-meaningful-routes-to-market/) [OLDER: Apr 2026]

### Inferences
- In a shrinking craft market, sellers are winning through direct, relationship-based channels (taprooms, clubs, pre-sales) rather than broad distribution. A members-first drop model fits that trend.
- Beer's small share of online alcohol (about 10%) points to a niche, community-driven model, not a mass-market e-commerce play.

## 5. Sentiment on limited-release "drops" and online can releases

### Takeaway
First-come online drops are widely linked to site crashes, sellouts within minutes, multi-account gaming and angry fans on social media. Lotteries and raffles, member pre-sales and waitlists are the fixes people point to as fair, especially when they favor local regulars over outside "bottle hunters." Lottery and allocation systems are now standard in 2026 for allocated spirits, and beer platforms have had lottery features for some time.

### Cited Findings
- Fixed-time drops cause crashes and "brutal" social media backlash and shut out people with busy schedules. CraftCellr added an automated, randomized lottery so the sale doesn't depend on who survives the traffic spike. — [American Craft Beer](https://www.americancraftbeer.com/new-app-feature-simplifies-beer-lottery-problems/); [Craft Brewing Business: CraftCellr lottery](https://www.craftbrewingbusiness.com/news/create-a-lottery-for-your-most-sought-after-beers-with-this-nifty-craftcellr-app-update/) [OLDER: multi-year-old, exact date not retrieved]
- Releases have sold out in 15–18 minutes. Buyers gamed limits with multiple emails, proxy friends and several phones. Breweries turned to pre-sales, waitlists and raffles. — [Arryved](https://www.arryved.com/blog/how-online-sales-helped-cabarrus-brewing-company/); [Craft Brewing Business](https://www.craftbrewingbusiness.com/featured/the-online-beer-sales-revolution-has-begun-get-to-know-these-key-platforms/) [OLDER]
- Hype releases such as Heady Topper and Pliny the Younger historically drew lines and sold out by lunchtime. They show the level of demand that online drops inherit. — [Wikipedia: Heady Topper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heady_Topper); [Wikipedia: Pliny the Younger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pliny_the_Younger_(beer)) [reference]
- **2026 lottery norms in adjacent alcohol categories:**
  - Maine BABLO ran an online spirits lottery (Feb 9–19, 2026).
  - Virginia ABC offered online lotteries (May 2026) and states that entry timing gives no advantage.
  - Montgomery County, MD runs an allocated-products program for retail customers.

  These systems are becoming familiar to consumers. — [Maine Spirits 2026 lottery](https://www.mainespirits.com/2026-limited-release-spirits-lottery); [Virginia ABC lottery](https://www.abc.virginia.gov/products/limited-availability/lottery); [Montgomery County ABS](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/alcohol-beverage-services/retail/highly-allocated-products-retail-customers) [OLDER: Feb–May 2026]
- Raffles "reward regular customers, as compared to systems that attract bottle hunters from outside the area." — [MarketWatch Mag: Managing the Hunt](https://www.marketwatchmag.com/managing-the-hunt/) [DATE UNCLEAR]
- Brewery membership models (mug clubs, bottle clubs) give members first access to pre-sales, renewal priority and waitlists. Tavour-run bottle clubs ship twice a year (Pure Project 2026). — [Throwback Brewery](https://www.throwbackbrewery.com/about/mug-club/); [Brewpublic](https://brewpublic.com/beer-releases/pure-project-expands-its-2026-bottle-club-with-tavour/) [OLDER: early 2026]
- A BeerAdvocate community thread on mug and beer clubs exists, but its content and date were not retrieved. — [BeerAdvocate thread](https://www.beeradvocate.com/community/threads/mug-clubs-beer-clubs-at-breweries.563577/) [DATE UNCLEAR]

### Inferences
- For The Chill Bottle Shop, a reserve-before-arrival allocation with a defined entry window, randomized or member-tier priority, one entry per verified member ID and pickup at the counter would address the main complaints about drops: crashes, bots and multiple accounts, and inaccessible time slots.
- Verified membership tied to ID doubles as bot and scalper control and as age verification. That fixes two pain points with one mechanism.
- Oʻahu's small, local market makes "reward the regulars" framing especially credible. Messaging should stress fairness and openness about how allocations are decided.

### Gaps
- I found no 2026 reporting on specific beer-drop website crashes or queue-system failures. Reddit and r/beertrade were not accessible.
- I found no data comparing consumer preference for lottery versus first-come in beer specifically.
- I found no Hawaii-specific coverage of drop or release culture. Search found no r/Hawaii or r/HonoluluHI threads.
