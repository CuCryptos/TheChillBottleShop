## Who are the competitors on Oʻahu (bottle shops, grocers, taprooms, delivery apps)?

### Takeaway
Oʻahu's specialist craft beer retail is a handful of independents: Village Bottle Shop & Tasting Room, Bev Mart, Tamura's, Young's Wine & Spirits and Off The Wall Craft Beer & Wine. Behind them are grocers such as Whole Foods and Foodland. Alcohol delivery already runs through DoorDash and Uber Eats, mostly from big chains (Safeway, Longs, Walgreens, H Mart). I found no evidence of a members-first, craft-curated delivery service on Oʻahu. That appears to be an open niche, but my search was limited and I have not confirmed it.

### Inferences
- The specialist competitor set is small and bricks-and-mortar. Village Bottle Shop is the closest match in positioning (curated, craft-first, strong on local beer). Bev Mart competes on price.

- Grocers (Foodland, Whole Foods, Safeway) will anchor price expectations for mainstream local six-packs.

## Oʻahu craft brewery landscape (2025–2026) and Hawaii production/economic data

### Takeaway
According to Brewers Association 2025 data, Hawaii has about 27 craft breweries, 48th among states. Production per adult is high: about 5 gallons per 21+ adult, 9th nationally. Maui Brewing and Kona Brewing are in the national top 50. On Oʻahu, 2025 brought consolidation and relocation: Aloha Beer shrank its taprooms and took over Waikīkī Brewing's plant, Honolulu Beerworks moved to Kalihi, and Kalihi Beer became Olohana. There were also a few new openings, such as Lēʻahi Brewing.

## Pricing realities: shelf prices, liquor tax, freight, freshness

### Takeaway
Hawaii's beer tax is among the highest in the US: $0.93 per gallon for packaged beer and $0.54 per gallon for draft, about 3rd highest per Tax Foundation. Mainland craft costs noticeably more than local craft on the shelf, reportedly about $16–18 per six-pack against $10–14 for Maui Brewing, because of container freight. Getting fresh hoppy mainland beer onto the shelf is a structural cost and logistics problem.

### Cited Findings
- **Liquor tax (HRS §244D):** $0.54 per wine gallon on draft beer (containers of 7 gallons or more) and $0.93 per wine gallon on other beer. — [Hawaii Dept of Taxation, HRS 244D](https://files.hawaii.gov/tax/legal/hrs/hrs_244d.pdf); [SalesTaxHandbook](https://www.salestaxhandbook.com/hawaii/alcohol) [OLDER: 2025]
- Hawaii ranks about 3rd highest among states for beer tax at $0.93 per gallon. [OLDER: 2026 edition, month unknown] — [Tax Foundation, Beer Taxes by State 2026](https://taxfoundation.org/data/all/state/beer-taxes-by-state/)
- A 2026 bill, **HB1991 HD2**, deals with liquor tax. Its contents could not be read because the capitol site was blocked. Check whether it changed rates and whether it passed. [OLDER: 2026 session] — [HB1991 HD2](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/sessions/session2026/bills/HB1991_HD2_.pdf)
- The Hawaii Department of Taxation publishes monthly liquor tax collection reports (for example, September 2025). [OLDER: 2025] — [DoTax monthly liquor stats](https://files.hawaii.gov/tax/stats/monthly/202509liq.pdf)
- **Reported shelf prices:**
  - Taproom pints are $8–12 and flights $12–18.
  - A grocery six-pack of Maui Brewing is about $10–14; a comparable mainland IPA "might hit $16–18."
  - Mainland craft "gets expensive in Hawaiʻi because everything ships by container."
  - [UNDATED; travel-guide source, moderate reliability] — [Hawaii-Guide.com](https://www.hawaii-guide.com/blog/hawaii-craft-beer-brewery-scene-what-to-drink)
- **Freight:**
  - Shipping beer between Hawaii and the mainland involves port handling, a refrigerated container, ocean freight, docking and transfer fees, and a distributor warehouse at the other end.
  - A pallet of grain costs about $100 to ship on the mainland versus $550–600 to Hawaii.
  - [UNDATED] — [VinePair](https://vinepair.com/articles/best-hawaii-craft-beer/)
- Matson household-goods quotes are about $4,800–5,400 for a 20 ft container and $7,200–8,300 for a 40 ft container, Oakland to Honolulu door to door. These are forum-reported household rates, not commercial reefer rates. [UNDATED forum post] — [City-Data Forum](https://www.city-data.com/forum/maui/1888249-new-shipping-cost-info-matson.html)
- Local beer is described as fresher and usually cheaper than mainland imports in Hawaii. [UNDATED] — [Hawaii-Guide.com](https://www.hawaii-guide.com/blog/hawaii-craft-beer-brewery-scene-what-to-drink)

### Inferences
- Rough math: the $0.93 per gallon tax on a 12-oz six-pack (0.5625 gal) is about $0.52 per six-pack. The tax is small next to the freight and distributor markup that create a mainland-versus-local price gap of about $4–6 per six-pack.
- A members-first model could lean on local beer (fresher, cheaper, "local kine" appeal) and treat mainland beer as limited, pre-ordered drops that justify cold-chain cost.

### Gaps
- I found no verified commercial refrigerated-container (reefer) rates for Matson or Pasha to Honolulu in 2025–26, and no sourced analysis of the Jones Act's effect on beer prices.
- I found no systematic price survey comparing Honolulu and mainland craft beer prices, and no BLS or C2ER cost-of-living figure specific to beer.
- Transit times from the West Coast to Honolulu and typical shelf age of mainland IPAs were not sourced.

## Recent (2025–2026) Hawaii news on liquor laws, delivery, taxes, retail

### Takeaway
Honolulu Liquor Commission rules already let retail dealers deliver alcohol against internet orders, with a signed receipt from a verified 21+ recipient. Curbside delivery is allowed under the delivery rules, but drive-in sales are banned. A 2025–26 bill, SB976, would allow direct-to-consumer shipping by beer and spirits manufacturers, including out-of-state ones. Its status is unclear: sources give conflicting effective dates. I found no September 2026 liquor law news.

### Cited Findings
- **Honolulu delivery rules:**
  - Retail licensees may deliver to homes or businesses on a bona fide order, which can be taken "by telephone, fax, or through the internet."
  - Delivery requires a signed receipt from a person verified to be 21+.
  - Relevant sections: Rules §3-82-36.4 and §3-82.1-31.15.
  - [Rule book revised 03-2018, posted Jan 2023: OLDER] — [Honolulu Liquor Commission Rules](https://www.honolulu.gov/liq/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2024/01/LIQ_Rule_Book_Rev_03-2018_Print_012023.pdf); [Insider Liquor Compliance, Mar 25, 2025](https://insiderliquorcompliance.com/2025/03/25/liquor-laws-rules-honolulu-liquor-commission/)
- **Curbside:** drive-in sales, where a customer orders from a car and is served there, are prohibited at retail premises unless the commission approves. However, "liquor delivered in accordance with delivery rules may be delivered to a customer in a vehicle at a curbside or other designated location." This bears directly on the Chill Bottle Shop's pickup model. [OLDER] — [Honolulu Liquor Commission Rules](https://www.honolulu.gov/liq/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2024/01/LIQ_Rule_Book_Rev_03-2018_Print_012023.pdf)
- License classes are defined in HRS §281-31. [OLDER: 2025] — [Justia HRS 281-31](https://law.justia.com/codes/hawaii/title-16/chapter-281/section-281-31/); [Insider Liquor Compliance license guide](https://insiderliquorcompliance.com/2025/03/23/what-are-the-different-types-of-liquor-licenses-in-hawaii/)
- **SB976 (direct shipment of beer and spirits):**
  - Would create a direct shipper permit for holders of a Class 1, 14 or 18 manufacturer license or an out-of-state manufacturer license, shipping to adults 21+ with an adult signature required.
  - It passed Second Reading as SD1 and was referred to the Judiciary Committee (JDC).
  - The bill carried over into the 2026 session folder.
  - Effective date is **conflicting**: one summary says July 1, 2025; another says July 1, 2050. The 2050 date is a common Hawaii placeholder that signals a bill still in play or dead, not enacted.
  - Industry was split over underage access and tax collection.
  - [OLDER: 2025–2026] — [SB976 SD1 (2026 session)](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/sessions/session2026/bills/SB976_SD1_.HTM); [BillTrack50](https://www.billtrack50.com/billdetail/1792362); [CitizenPortal](https://citizenportal.ai/articles/6364193/Hawaii/Legislative/Senate/Senate-Committee-on-Commerce-and-Consumer-Protection/Senate-committees-recommend-passage-of-bill-to-allow-direct-shipment-of-beer-and-spirits-amid-industry-split-over-underage-access-and-tax-collection)

- Civil Beat covered the Honolulu Liquor Commission's new administrator and planned changes. [OLDER: Nov 2023] — [Civil Beat](https://www.civilbeat.org/2023/11/the-honolulu-liquor-commission-has-a-new-team-will-it-help/)

### Inferences
- The current Honolulu rules already fit an online-order, delivery and curbside-pickup model for a retail dealer (Class 4) licensee, subject to age verification and signed receipts.
- If SB976 (or a successor) becomes law, mainland breweries could ship straight to Oʻahu consumers. That would be a new competitor for rare mainland beer, but freight costs would still limit it.
