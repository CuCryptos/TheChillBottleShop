## 1. How do successful brewery/bottle-shop membership programs work (pricing, perks, allocations, renewal/wait lists)?

### Takeaway
Top-tier brewery "bottle societies" in 2025–2026 cluster at **$325–$700/yr** and are mostly **prepaid beer credit/included allocations** (the fee buys beer, not just access), with invite-only or right-of-first-refusal renewal creating scarcity. Pure "access-only" mug clubs sit around **~$100/yr** and justify the fee with discounts, pours, and swag. The Chill Bottle Shop's $75/$150 access-fee tiers sit at the mug-club end of the market, so perceived value must come from reliable access, not included beer.

### Cited Findings
- **Firestone Walker Brewmaster's Collective 2026:** $399 + tax annual prepaid; members receive five packages ("drops") across the year. [>30d; 2026 program page] — [Firestone Walker](https://www.firestonewalker.com/brewmasters-collective/)
- **The Bruery Reserve Society 2026:** $325 (+tax) yearly fee; enrolls for a limited time; reported to include up to ~$450 worth of beer annually (i.e., fee is more than returned as beer). [>30d] — [The Bruery Reserve Society](https://www.thebruery.com/pages/the-reserve-society); [My BeerBuzz: Reserve Society 2026 details](https://mybeerbuzz.com/the-bruery-announces-reserve-society-2026-details/); value figure via [Bruery memberships page](https://www.thebruery.com/collections/bruery-memberships) (search snippet)
- **The Bruery Hoarders Society 2026:** $695/yr, **invite-only**; invitations emailed just before renewal; access to 50+ Society-exclusive selections plus 7 Hoarders-only releases. Tiered structure: Reserve Society acts as the feeder/waitlist into Hoarders. [>30d] — [The Bruery Hoarders Society](https://www.thebruery.com/pages/society-hoarders); [Society Terms & Conditions](https://www.thebruery.com/pages/society-terms-conditions)
- **Sierra Nevada Friends of the Family:** $499; 36 beers (3 each of 12 brews) in six drops. [date not verified] — [Hop Culture, "20 Best Brewery Memberships"](https://www.hopculture.com/best-brewery-membership-bottle-societies/)
- **Horus Aged Ales:** two tiers, Sky God $600 and Crowned Eagle $400, each including 12 bottles plus opportunity to buy more; **the most limited releases are allocated to the higher (Sky God) tier first**; member allocation per release depends on total bottles produced. [date not verified] — [Hop Culture](https://www.hopculture.com/best-brewery-membership-bottle-societies/) (via search snippet)
- **Forbes (Nov 8, 2025)** ran "8 Brewery Memberships With The Most Exclusive Beers" — confirms the category is active in 2025–26 (page content not retrievable). [>30d] — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/dontse/2025/11/08/8-brewery-memberships-with-the-most-exclusive-beers/)
- **Side Project Brewing La Coterie:** membership gives members access to **reserve and purchase** select beers before public sale; 2026 term runs March 2026–Feb 2027. Price not surfaced in search. This is the closest analog to Chill Bottle Shop's "pay for access, then buy" model. [>30d] — [Side Project La Coterie](https://www.sideprojectbrewing.com/pages/la-coterie)
- **Hill Farmstead "Collected Works" (historical, 2013):** $350 for 12 bottles (some chosen by member from list of 15), wooden crate, 4 glasses, **automatic right of first refusal for next round** and festival entry. [>30d — 2013, illustrative only] — [mybeerbuzz 2013](https://mybeerbuzz.blogspot.com/2013/02/hill-farmstead-collected-works-private.html)
- **Mug club benchmark:** Ship Bottom Brewery 2026 Mug Club yearly fee $99. [>30d] — [Ship Bottom Brewery](https://shipbottombrewery.com/our-shop/ship-bottom-brewery-2026-mug-club-membership/)
- **Access-plus-perks example:** Boomtown Brewery Golden Class $325, capped at first 100 buyers: growler + fill, 10 free pints/month, glass, hat, early access to special release events, 20% off. [date not verified] — [HomebrewTalk list of brewery membership clubs](https://www.homebrewtalk.com/threads/list-of-brewery-membership-clubs.706884/page-4)
- **Russian River Pliny the Younger 2026 (non-membership, in-person model):** March 20–April 2, 2026; first-come in person; each guest allotted three 10-oz pours and three bottles to go; bottle allocations transferable within the party; waits of 6+ hours, lines from 5–6 a.m. [>30d, Mar 2026] — [Press Democrat, Mar 10 2026](https://www.pressdemocrat.com/2026/03/10/pliny-the-younger-returns-soon-what-to-know-about-the-2026-release/); [Russian River FAQ](https://www.russianriverbrewing.com/frequently-asked-questions/)
- **Cigar City Hunahpu's Day 2026:** scaled back to a free, no-ticket taproom event (Mar 13, 2026) with bottle releases from 11 a.m. — a retreat from the old ticketed festival model (earlier coverage titled "The Death of Hunahpu's Day"; festival was once canceled). [>30d] — [Cigar City event page](https://cigarcitybrewing.com/event/hunahpus-day-2026-tbbw-2026-03-13/); [All About Beer](https://allaboutbeer.com/hunahpus-day/); [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/cigar-city-cancels-hunahpus-day-festival/)
- **Toppling Goliath KBBS:** sold only at the annual brewery release, using a **lottery** (e.g., 2022 10th-anniversary lottery sign-up). [>30d] — [Toppling Goliath KBBS page](https://www.tgbrews.com/kbbs); [TG on X, 2022](https://x.com/TGBrews/status/1583184540528693248)

### Inferences
- Two dominant models: (a) **prepaid-beer societies** ($325–$700, fee ≈ or < beer value delivered) and (b) **access/perk clubs** (~$99–$325 with discounts, pours, swag). Chill Bottle Shop's model (fee buys access; beer purchased separately) is closest to Side Project La Coterie and Tavour+ ($119, see §2) — and Tavour+ shows the risk: if access doesn't reliably convert into beer, members feel cheated.
- Tiering by allocation priority (Horus: top tier gets rarest bottles first; Bruery: Reserve → invite to Hoarders) is an established precedent for Founding vs Member early-access windows.
- Right-of-first-refusal renewal and capped membership counts (Boomtown first 100; Hoarders invite-only) are common scarcity levers; a capped Founding tier with guaranteed renewal is well-precedented.
- Consider crediting part of the fee back as beer/store credit (e.g., $X of the $150 as credit) to move toward the "fee returns as beer" norm — this is an inference, not a sourced benchmark.

### Gaps
- Current 2026 terms for Tree House, Hill Farmstead, Monkish, Other Half, Trillium, The Veil, Cellarmaker, Jester King, Fonta Flora could not be retrieved (searches returned no membership details; fetches blocked). Several of these (Tree House, Monkish, Trillium, Other Half) appear to sell via **online pre-order/pickup windows without paid membership** — unverified.
- No hard data on member counts, renewal rates, or waitlist lengths for any brewery society.
- Side Project La Coterie 2026 price not surfaced.
- No Hawaiʻi or bottle-shop (vs brewery) membership programs found.

## 2. Subscription boxes/clubs: pricing, churn/retention benchmarks, why people cancel

### Takeaway
Consumer subscription churn runs roughly **6.5–8.5%/month** for consumer goods; wine clubs lose **~20–36%/yr** with ~40% of new members cancelling in year one. Beer-specific cancellation complaints center on **paying for "early access" that doesn't actually secure beer**, shipping delays/freshness, and damaged product.

### Cited Findings
- **Tavour (2026):** no membership fee; beers ~$7.50–$11.50 each; flat $14.90 shipping per crate. **Tavour+** is invite-only, **$119/yr**: early access to every release, member-exclusive beers, free shipping on crates of 12+. [date not verified; product page current] — [Tavour+](https://www.tavour.com/tavour-plus); [Tavour+ FAQ](https://www.tavour.com/frequently-asked-questions-tavour-plus); [Beer Thrillers, Oct 2024 [>30d]](https://thebeerthrillers.com/2024/10/01/tavour-your-guide-to-craft-beer-delivered-right-to-your-door/)
- **Why Tavour users cancel:** Tavour+ members complained promised first access wasn't delivered — beers showed sold out immediately after notification, so membership had "no perceived benefit"; 20+ day ship delays with freshness concerns; damaged/flat beers; poor customer service; inflexible single ship day. [complaint dates not verified] — [BeerAdvocate "Issues with Tavour"](https://www.beeradvocate.com/community/threads/issues-with-tavour.337231/); [BBB Tavour complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/wa/seattle/profile/wholesale-beer/tavour-beer-1296-90004708/complaints); [JustUseApp reviews 2026](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/956371806/tavour/reviews)
- **Recurly benchmarks:** overall subscription churn ~3.27%/month (voluntary 2.41%, involuntary 0.86%); consumer goods/digital media ~6.5%; most consumer subscription brands ~6.5–8.5%/month; voluntary churn mostly price-related; up to 70% of involuntary churn from failed payments. [date not verified; some figures via secondary aggregators] — [Recurly churn rate benchmarks](https://recurly.com/research/churn-rate-benchmarks/); [Subjolt Churn Benchmarks 2026](https://www.subjolt.com/guides/churn-rate-benchmarks/)
- **Wine club churn:** annual churn 20–25% at many wineries; average tenure fell from 36 to 30 months; other estimates 28–36%/yr attrition (luxury 23–29%); **~40% of new members cancel within first year**; promo-period joiners show 42% higher early cancellation than tasting-room joiners; wine club retention 64–77% vs country/golf clubs 92–94%. Sources are marketing/consulting blogs citing SVB DtC data — treat as secondary. [Wine Industry Advisor Apr 21 2026 [>30d]; others date not verified] — [Wine Industry Advisor, "The Wine Club Retention Crisis"](https://wineindustryadvisor.com/2026/04/21/the-wine-club-retention-crisis/); [Private Club Marketing](https://privateclubmarketing.com/wine-club-membership-growth-retention-strategy/); [Highway 29 Creative 2025](https://www.hwy29creative.com/blog/wine-club-retention-strategies-2025)

### Inferences
- The Tavour+ complaint is the single most relevant warning: a paid "early access" tier that still sells out instantly is perceived as worthless. Chill Bottle Shop's tier windows must be sized so a meaningful share of paying members actually secure beer (or guarantee a minimum allocation per drop / per year).
- Annual billing (vs monthly) reduces the number of churn decision points; the renewal date is the key moment — send a year-in-review ("you reserved X drops, saved $Y") before renewal.
- Promo-driven sign-ups churn faster; favor organic/in-store sign-ups for Founding tier.
- Involuntary churn: use card updater/dunning for annual renewals.

### Gaps
- No beer-specific churn benchmark found (Craft Beer Club, Beer of the Month Club, Hop Culture box, brewery can clubs pricing not retrieved).
- No first-party Recurly or WineDirect report read directly (fetch blocked).

## 3. Allocation fairness: lottery vs first-come vs tiered vs pre-order; bots, crashes, fixes; consumer perceptions

### Takeaway
Pure first-come online drops produce crashes and "sold out instantly" resentment; the industry's main fixes are **lotteries/raffles** (Bruery since 2014, Toppling Goliath KBBS, CraftCellr lottery tool), **per-person limits**, **member-only windows**, and **tiered priority**. Lotteries are framed as fairer to people who can't be at a computer at the release minute.

### Cited Findings
- High-demand online releases can crash brewery sites and trigger social media backlash from fans who feel wronged. [date not verified, ~2020] — [American Craft Beer](https://www.americancraftbeer.com/new-app-feature-simplifies-beer-lottery-problems/)
- **The Bruery switched to a lottery in 2014** to be fair to people who can't get to a computer at the exact release time and to keep the website from crashing. [>30d] — [American Craft Beer](https://www.americancraftbeer.com/new-app-feature-simplifies-beer-lottery-problems/)
- **CraftCellr (Oznr) lottery feature (June 2020):** fully automated randomized purchase process for beers where demand exceeds supply, avoiding traffic-induced site failures. [>30d] — [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/craftcellr-announces-lottery-feature/); [Craft Brewing Business](https://www.craftbrewingbusiness.com/news/create-a-lottery-for-your-most-sought-after-beers-with-this-nifty-craftcellr-app-update/); [Oznr blog](https://oznr.com/blog/2020/6/15/craftcellr-announces-new-lottery-feature)
- **Cross-category precedent:** EQL (sneakers/spirits) runs verified raffles that enforce 1 item per person and filter bots, while keeping sites up under load; Pennsylvania liquor board uses lotteries against bourbon bots/scalpers. [dates not verified] — [EQL RunFair](https://www.eql.com/runfair); [Philadelphia Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/philly/news/PA-fighting-craft-bourbon-bots-and-craft-whiskey-scalpers-with-liquor-lottery.html); [Found North lottery FAQ](https://www.foundnorthwhisky.com/lottery-faq)
- **Tiered priority precedent:** Horus allocates the most limited releases to the higher-priced tier first. — [Hop Culture](https://www.hopculture.com/best-brewery-membership-bottle-societies/)
- **Per-person caps / transferability:** Russian River 2026 limits 3 bottles per guest, transferable within the party. [>30d] — [Press Democrat](https://www.pressdemocrat.com/2026/03/10/pliny-the-younger-returns-soon-what-to-know-about-the-2026-release/)
- **"Silent release"** tactic: Toppling Goliath once announced KBBS release at midnight for next-day opening to dampen crowds (2018). [>30d] — [TG on Twitter](https://twitter.com/tgbrews/status/982065380976943104?lang=en)

### Inferences
- For Chill Bottle Shop: first-come *within* tier windows works while supply ≥ member demand. Add a fallback: when a drop's reservations exceed supply within the Founding window (or for "hyper-limited" drops), switch to a **short entry window + random draw** among members, with Founding entries weighted or drawn first. This preserves tier value while removing the "who refreshed fastest" problem.
- Enforce 1 reservation per member per drop (account + payment card + delivery address matching) — a bot/scalper defense that small shops can do without Queue-it.
- A member-only, logged-in window is itself a strong anti-bot filter (paid accounts are costly to farm).
- Publish allocation rules and post-drop stats (units, members who got beer) to build trust.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 consumer survey on perceived fairness of lottery vs first-come found; Reddit/BeerAdvocate threads could not be retrieved.
- No Queue-it case study for a brewery found.

## 4. Pricing benchmarks for paid memberships (beer & wine) and perks that justify the fee

### Takeaway
Beer societies with included beer: $325–$700/yr. Access/perk clubs: ~$99–$325/yr. Invite-only access tier: Tavour+ $119/yr. Wine DtC shipments average ~$521/order and ~$57/bottle in 2025, so wine clubs are much higher-ticket. At $75/$150 with no included beer, Chill Bottle Shop sits at/below mug-club pricing, which is reasonable if perks are concrete.

### Cited Findings
- Beer benchmarks: Firestone $399 (5 drops); Bruery Reserve $325 (~$450 beer), Hoarders $695 invite-only; Sierra Nevada $499 (36 beers); Horus $400/$600 (12 bottles); Ship Bottom mug club $99; Boomtown $325 (pints, discount, early access); Tavour+ $119 (early access + free shipping). — sources as cited in §1 and §2.
- **Wine DtC (Sovos ShipCompliant/WineBusiness Analytics 2026 report, covering 2025):** avg bottle shipped direct record $56.78 (+11% YoY); avg order value $521; DtC channel volume −15% (−967k cases), value −6% in 2025. [>30d, early 2026] — [Sovos press release](https://sovos.com/press-releases/2026-direct-to-consumer-wine-shipping-report-reveals-record-declines-as-market-downturn-deepens/); [The Drinks Business, Jan 2026](https://www.thedrinksbusiness.com/2026/01/average-price-of-a-bottle-of-us-wine-hits-56-78/)
- Perks commonly bundled: early access/priority allocation, member-only beers, included bottles, discounts (e.g., 20% off), free pours, glassware/crate, event/festival entry, free shipping, right of first refusal for renewal. — [Hill Farmstead 2013](https://mybeerbuzz.blogspot.com/2013/02/hill-farmstead-collected-works-private.html); [HomebrewTalk](https://www.homebrewtalk.com/threads/list-of-brewery-membership-clubs.706884/page-4); [Tavour+](https://www.tavour.com/tavour-plus)

### Inferences
- To justify $150 Founding: guaranteed renewal/right of first refusal, capped count, first window, at least one Founding-only drop or bottle per year, glass/merch, free local delivery, events. To justify $75 Member: early window vs public, member pricing or free delivery threshold.
- A "guaranteed minimum" (e.g., Founding members guaranteed ≥1 unit of every drop they reserve in-window, or N allocations/year) directly counters the Tavour+ failure mode.
- Hawaiʻi shipping costs are high; free local delivery is likely a tangible, easily-valued perk (inference).

### Gaps
- No consumer willingness-to-pay data for beer memberships found.
- Wine club membership fee structures (most wine clubs charge per shipment, not an access fee) not detailed from primary sources.

## 5. Pitfalls: unclaimed allocations, no-shows, forfeiture, legal issues with prepaid memberships/allocations

### Takeaway
Brewery pickup windows range from same-day to ~7 days, with forfeiture policies ranging from full refund to 90% refund to no refund. Legal exposure centers on auto-renewal/negative-option laws (FTC Click-to-Cancel vacated July 2025 but ROSCA and ~30 state laws apply; FTC reviving rule in 2026) and Hawaiʻi liquor rules (delivery requires 21+ verified recipient; retail sales in original packages).

### Cited Findings
- **Pickup/forfeiture policies:** Alchemist — pick up within 48 hours, then contact for refund; Tree House — pick up same day or order may be refunded/restocked; El Segundo Brewing — held 7 days (pre-orders 7 days after release), then forfeited with no refund; Burial — 5 days, then restocked without notice, 90% refund on request; Highland Park — no refunds for bottles not picked up in the quarterly window. [dates not verified] — [Alchemist pickup](https://shop.alchemistbeer.com/in-person-curbside-pickup); [Tree House FAQ](https://treehousebrew.com/faqs); [El Segundo Pickup FAQ](https://www.elsegundobrewing.com/shipping-pickup-faq/); [Burial shipping/pickup conditions](https://burialbeer.com/pages/shipping-conditions); [Highland Park orders & refunds](https://hpb.la/shipping-pickup-faq/)
- **FTC Click-to-Cancel:** Eighth Circuit vacated the rule July 8, 2025; FTC moving to revive it in 2026 but as of mid-2026 no replacement in force; ROSCA still requires clear disclosure of material terms before billing, express informed consent, and a simple way to stop recurring charges. [Feb–May 2026, >30d] — [Arnold & Porter, Feb 2026](https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/advisories/2026/02/ftc-and-state-ags-continue-to-scrutinize-subscription-practices); [Jones Day, May 2026](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2026/05/ftc-revives-clicktocancel-rule-new-risks-for-subscription-businesses); [Goodwin, Feb 2026](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/02/alerts-practices-ba-ftcs-click-to-cancel-rule-gets-new-life)
- **State auto-renewal laws:** ~30 states have them, some exceeding the vacated federal rule; NYC rule effective Oct 1, 2026; Louisiana Click to Cancel Act effective Jan 1, 2027. [Summer 2026, >30d] — [Kelley Drye, Summer 2026 roundup](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/summer-2026-autorenewal-roundup-nyc-and-louisiana-enact-new-regulatory-requirements); [Adaptiv state guide](https://adaptivpayments.com/blog/automatic-renewal-laws-by-state)
- **Hawaiʻi liquor law:** retail license authorizes sale in original packages (HRS ch. 281); manufacturers/wholesalers may not extend credit to retailers beyond 30 days from invoice. [2025 HRS] — [Justia HRS §281-37](https://law.justia.com/codes/hawaii/title-16/chapter-281/section-281-37/); [Honolulu HRS 281 PDF](https://www.honolulu.gov/liq/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2024/04/HRS_281_072018_Website.pdf)
- **Honolulu delivery:** retail dealers may deliver to persons 21+ on a bona fide order; receipt signed by an age-verified adult at the delivery point; delivery hours for off-premises product 6 a.m.–midnight; retail delivery staff may be employees/agents 18+ (per rules). — [Honolulu Liquor Commission Rules](https://www.honolulu.gov/liq/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2024/01/LIQ_Rule_Book_Rev_03-2018_Print_012023.pdf); [Honolulu General Guide for Liquor Licenses, Mar 2025](https://www.honolulu.gov/liq/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2025/03/General-Guide-for-Liquor-Licenses.pdf)

### Inferences
- Recommended Chill Bottle Shop policy shape: charge at reservation (reduces no-shows), 7-day pickup hold after arrival notice with reminders, one paid extension or conversion to delivery, then forfeit with partial refund/store credit (e.g., 90% like Burial) — full no-refund policies risk goodwill and possibly consumer-protection complaints. Unclaimed units flow to waitlist/next tier.
- Track no-shows per member; repeated no-shows (e.g., 2+ per year) lose early-window priority — a common-sense pattern, not a sourced benchmark.
- Annual auto-renewing memberships should comply with ROSCA and California-style ARL practices (clear pre-checkout disclosure, affirmative consent, renewal reminder, online self-cancel) since the site may serve out-of-state visitors.
- Membership fee should be structured as payment for access/services, not prepayment for liquor, and the retailer should confirm with the Honolulu Liquor Commission whether taking payment before product is in the licensed premises (pre-sale reservations) or before the product is legally received from the wholesaler raises any issue. Hawaiʻi gift-card/prepaid rules (HRS ch. 481B) may apply if store credit is issued.

### Gaps
- **No source found** on whether Hawaiʻi/Honolulu rules restrict retail **pre-sales/reservations of liquor not yet in inventory**, or membership "clubs" tied to alcohol discounts; needs direct inquiry to the Honolulu Liquor Commission or a liquor attorney.
- No Hawaiʻi auto-renewal statute confirmed in search results (Hawaiʻi was not specifically named).
- No data on typical no-show rates for beer pre-orders.
